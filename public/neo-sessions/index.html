  • Sessions are the group meetings of the Culture and Learning workstream (the Tuesday Workforce Collaborative call and its in-person sessions), the Strategy and Growth workstream (the Friday Growth Team call and its hot seat sessions), the Ignition Peer Group, and the monthly community gathering.
